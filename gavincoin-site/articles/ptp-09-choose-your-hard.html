Paying the Price

Choose Your Hard

Last time: name what you're avoiding, then lean into it. That leads to a bigger truth every player eventually meets. There is no easy option. There's only a choice between two kinds of hard. Every road is hard. You just pick which hard. Doing the work is hard. Training when you're tired, staying disciplined, pushing through the boring reps — that's hard. But comfort has a cost, and that's hard too. Sitting on the bench because you didn't put the work in is hard. Watching teammates pass you by is hard. Regret is hard. So it was never a choice between hard and easy. It's the hard of effort now, or the hard of regret later.

Comfort has a cost. The comfortable choice always feels easier in the moment. That's the trap. The cost just shows up later, when it's harder to do anything about it. Am I paying the price, or paying the cost?

This week's challenge

Next time your player faces the comfortable option, put the real choice to them: "Do you want the hard of doing it now — or the hard of wishing you had?" In the last email: what happens after you start winning.
