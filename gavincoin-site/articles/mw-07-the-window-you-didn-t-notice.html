Mirrors & Windows — The Power of Perspective

The Window You Didn't Notice

Most players understand the danger of worrying about the opposition. Far fewer notice how often they compare themselves to their own teammate. "She's fitter than me. He always gets picked. Coach trusts them more than me."

Comparison doesn't make the team stronger. Contribution does. It starts quietly. Without realising it, attention drifts from "how can I help the team?" to "why am I not like them?" It's still the window — the direction just changed. The problem didn't. I've watched talented players slowly disappear from matches this way. Not because they became worse players — because comparison stole their attention. They stopped demanding the ball, stopped making runs, stopped backing themselves. Every team needs different strengths. Some lead with their voice, some with work rate. Not everyone contributes the same way. They just need to contribute.

This week's challenge

Ask your player this week: "What's one way you can help this week that's just yours — not anyone else's?"
