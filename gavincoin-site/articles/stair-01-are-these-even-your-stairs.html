The Stairs

Are These Even Your Stairs?

Most goal-setting advice starts with the same instruction: aim high, picture the top, want it badly enough. But I think there's a question that has to come first — long before we count the steps. Are these even your stairs?

A goal you didn't choose is a staircase you'll never want to climb. So often, a young player is climbing towards something that was never really theirs. A goal a parent set. A goal borrowed from the teammate they keep comparing themselves to. A goal picked because it looked impressive, not because it mattered to them. And then everyone's confused about why the motivation keeps running out. It runs out because the staircase belongs to someone else.

The first step is ownership. Inside Unbeatable, one of our core philosophies is The Stairs — and before we break a single goal into steps, we ask the player one thing: Is this staircase yours? Because a goal you actually chose is one you'll get back up and keep climbing, even on the hard days. What's my next step? (But first — whose step is it?)

This week's challenge

Ask your player to name one goal they're working towards. Then ask them, gently: "Is that yours — or someone else's?" The honest answer changes everything that comes next.
