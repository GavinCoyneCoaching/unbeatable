The Jar — Why Organisation Matters

If everything is a priority, nothing is

If sand is the problem, what are the rocks? It's easy to point out the distractions getting in the way. The harder question is what actually deserves to go into the jar first — and that's where most people get stuck. Not because they don't know what matters, but because they've never stopped to identify it. Rocks are the things that genuinely move your life forward. For a young player: training, recovery, sleep, school, family. For a coach: preparation, communication, culture, leadership. Everyone's rocks are different — but everyone has them.

If everything is a priority, nothing is. The trap is trying to treat everything like a rock. Pebbles still matter — extra skill work, courses, personal development — but they don't go in before the rocks. Deep down, we usually know what our rocks are. The challenge is that rocks are hard and sand is easy. So we drift toward sand — not because we don't know better, but because it asks less of us.

This week's challenge

Ask your player — or ask yourself: "What are your three biggest rocks right now?" Not what should be. What actually matters most. Write the three down. Protect them first.
