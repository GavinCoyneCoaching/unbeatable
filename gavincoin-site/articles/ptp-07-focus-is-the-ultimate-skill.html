Paying the Price

Focus Is The Ultimate Skill

Last time: kind of effort gets kind of results — full attention is what actually fills the cup. So here's the question underneath that: what decides whether a player shows up kind of, or shows up fully?

Ability isn't fixed. It grows — and focus is what grows it. We talk about ability like it's handed out at birth — you either have it or you don't. The longer I do this, the less I believe that. Ability develops. And the single biggest factor in how much it develops is focus. A player who focuses better learns faster. Not because focus replaces ability — because it develops it. Every rep done with real attention teaches the body and the brain something a rep done half-present never will.

Which changes the question worth asking. In any sport, only a small number ever become elite. That's just true, and pretending otherwise doesn't help anyone. But that was never the real question. The real one is: "Am I getting the best from my ability?" Same as the cup. It was never about whose cup is bigger. It's about how full you fill yours. Am I paying the price, or paying the cost?

This week's challenge

This week, help your player swap one question for another: "Am I the best?" depends on everyone else. "Am I getting the best from myself?" depends only on them. Ask the second one.
