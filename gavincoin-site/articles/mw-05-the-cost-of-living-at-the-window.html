Mirrors & Windows — The Power of Perspective

The Cost Of Living At The Window

A decision goes against your player. A teammate slips. The opposition scores. They glance out the window — and never come back. Still on the pitch, but mentally, they've stepped away from the game.

Performance doesn't happen where your attention isn't. Here's what's worth remembering: nothing outside the window has to be bad. The referee isn't the problem. The opposition isn't the problem. The weather isn't the problem. The problem is only when attention stays there. This isn't about ignoring what's around you. Good players notice. Great players know when to come back. A glance is useful. Living there isn't.

This week's challenge

After the next game, name one moment together — just one — where your player's attention got stuck at the window. Not to criticise it. Just to notice it out loud.
