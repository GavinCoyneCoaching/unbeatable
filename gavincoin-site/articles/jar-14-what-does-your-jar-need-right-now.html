The Jar — Why Organisation Matters

What does your jar need right now?

We've spent a good few weeks on The Jar now — the ping-pong balls, the busy-versus- productive lesson, the honest look at what actually fills your day, the sand, the rocks, the right- now question, proportion, the sideline reset, the empty jar, the half-full jar, the five-gallon bucket, every yes requiring a no, and the truth that nobody fills your jar but you. This weekend, not another lesson. A challenge.

The point was never a perfect jar. It's awareness. Take ten minutes, a notebook, or just a quiet moment, and ask one question: what does my jar need right now? Maybe it's overflowing — too many commitments, too much noise. Then your job isn't to add. It's to empty some sand out. Maybe it's full of the wrong things — lots of movement, little progress — and it needs reorganising: what have you treated like sand that's really a rock? Maybe it's half-full and healthy, and simply needs protecting. Or maybe it's surprisingly empty, and needs one rock — one goal worth moving towards. The point isn't perfection. It's awareness. Because once you can see what's in the jar, you can improve it. And the biggest breakthroughs rarely come from working harder — they come from becoming clearer.
