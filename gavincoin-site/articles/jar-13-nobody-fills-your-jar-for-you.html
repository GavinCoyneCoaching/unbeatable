The Jar — Why Organisation Matters

Nobody fills your jar for you

I hear it constantly — from players, and honestly from adults too. "I've no time." "My schedule's mad." "There's just too much going on." All said like the jar filled itself. Like it was done to them. Some of that is real — life is genuinely busy, genuinely full of things outside your control. But underneath most of it is a quieter truth worth sitting with.

You don't control how big your jar is. You control what goes in it. Nobody else can put your rocks in first for you. Nobody else can pull the sand out. A coach can point out what the rocks probably are. A parent can protect the schedule around them. But the choosing — moment to moment, yes by yes — that part is yours. It's not a one-off decision you make and forget. It's a daily discipline: keep asking what deserves your attention, keep checking what actually went in. That's really what sits underneath everything in this philosophy. Not "how do I get a bigger jar" — you won't. Just: what deserves my attention, and am I the one deciding, or is everything else deciding for me?

This week's challenge

Next time you catch yourself saying "I've no time," stop and ask instead: "What did I actually choose to let in today?" Own the answer, whatever it is. In the final email: what to do with everything we've covered — one question, one weekend, ten minutes.
