Mirrors & Windows — The Power of Perspective

Why Teams Collapse Under Pressure

You've watched a team with real ability fall apart under pressure — arguing, blaming, panic after mistakes — despite having plenty of talent on the pitch. Talent didn't fail them. Focus did. When the heat comes on, players stop looking at what they can control and start staring at everything they can't. Too long at the window and they lose focus on their own role, effort and responsibility. Start with the mirror instead, and something shifts — accountability improves, communication improves, teams get calmer and more supportive under pressure. We've already seen it in practice: less fighting between teammates, better reactions after mistakes, stronger communication. Not because the players suddenly became more talented — because they became more focused.

This week's challenge

Notice one mirror-first moment from your team this week — a player who came back quickly after a mistake — and name it out loud to them.
