Focus

The Window Never Closes

A lot of players are waiting for the day the distractions disappear. When the tough opponent, the loud sideline, the bad call, the nagging doubt all just… stop. They're going to be waiting a long time.

The window never closes. There will always be another distraction. Another criticism. Another stronger opponent. Another thing outside your control clamouring for your attention. The goal was never to remove the windows. That's impossible. The goal is to stop letting them dictate your focus. Once a player stops waiting for perfect, quiet conditions and accepts that the window is always there, something shifts. They stop being surprised by distraction — and start choosing where their attention goes despite it. That's real focus. Not the absence of distraction. The refusal to be run by it.

This week's challenge

Before the next game, set the expectation with your player: "There'll be distractions today. That's normal. Where are you choosing to put your attention anyway?" Where is my attention right now?
