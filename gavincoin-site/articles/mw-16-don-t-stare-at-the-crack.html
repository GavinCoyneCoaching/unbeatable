Mirrors & Windows — The Power of Perspective

Don't Stare At The Crack

One mistake, and a player decides they're a poor player. One missed shot, and they stop asking for the ball. A crack changes part of the picture. It doesn't remove the picture. Imagine a mirror with a single crack across it. Can it still show your reflection? Of course — the crack changes part of the picture, it doesn't erase it. Yet that's exactly what players do to themselves after a mistake. Recognising a weakness isn't the problem — every player has them. The problem is when one weakness becomes the only thing you can see. Attention gets trapped: replaying the missed tackle instead of noticing good positioning, reliving five minutes ago instead of reacting to now. Notice the crack. Learn from it. Then step back and see the whole mirror.

This week's challenge

If your player fixates on one mistake this week, hand them the line: "That's a crack, not the whole mirror."
