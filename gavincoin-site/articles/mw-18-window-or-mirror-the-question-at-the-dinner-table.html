Mirrors & Windows — The Power of Perspective

Window Or Mirror — The Question At The Dinner Table

The car ride home, or the dinner table after — most of us fall into the same pattern of questions. How did it go? Did you win? What happened? All of it pointed outward, at the result.

"Today, are we going to spend more time looking out the window or in the mirror?" That's a question worth asking at the start of the day, not just after it. Not about the score, not about the referee, not about who's playing well — just where the day's attention is actually going to go. It sits alongside the same small reflection habits we use elsewhere in this programme: "What would the mirror say?" "How quickly did I return to what I can influence?" None of them need a long conversation. They just need to be asked, regularly, until they become the player's own question rather than yours.

This week's challenge

Ask the exact question once this week — in the car, or at the table — and just listen to what comes back: "Today, are we going to spend more time looking out the window or in the mirror?"
