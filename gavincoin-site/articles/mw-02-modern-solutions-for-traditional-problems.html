Mirrors & Windows — The Power of Perspective

Modern Solutions For Traditional Problems

Picture your player after a match, head down over a phone, scrolling — comparing themselves to a highlight reel, a teammate's post, someone else's "day." The problems young players face today are, at their core, the same ones players have always faced. Pressure. Fear of mistakes. Worry about letting teammates down. Nothing new there.

The problems haven't changed. The noise around them has. What's changed is the environment. Years ago the pressure came from the people in front of you — the coach, the opposition, the crowd. Now it comes from far further away: social media, comparison, the constant feeling of being watched and measured. Our players are looking out the window at more noise than any generation before them. That's why modern players often need modern solutions. Not lower standards, not less competitiveness — a skill we used to leave to chance: how to bring attention back inward under pressure. We coach the solo and the tackle. It's time we coached the refocus too.

This week's challenge

Ask your player one honest question this week: "What's pulling your attention that has nothing to do with the actual game?" You might be surprised what they name.
