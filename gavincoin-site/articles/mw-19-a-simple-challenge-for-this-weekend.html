Mirrors & Windows — The Power of Perspective

A Simple Challenge For This Weekend

Understanding an idea is useful. Applying it is where the value lives. So here's a simple challenge for this weekend — whether you're a player, coach or parent. The goal isn't perfection. It's awareness. See if you can spot the windows. A referee gives a decision you don't agree with — window. A teammate makes a mistake — window. The opposition scores — window. You start worrying about the scoreboard — window. Every time you catch yourself looking out, ask one question: "What would the mirror say?" What can I control here? What's my next job? How can I help? How can I respond? Coaches — the simplest thing you can do this weekend is use the language. When heads drop: "Are we looking in the mirror or out the window?" The goal isn't perfection. It's awareness. Once players notice where their focus has drifted, they can bring it back — and the teams that bring it back quickest tend to stay in matches longest.

This week's challenge

Spot three windows this weekend — out loud, if you can — then bring each one home to the mirror. In the final email: the one line that ties this whole series together.
