Focus

What Are You Feeding?

Attention works a lot like feeding. Whatever you feed, grows.

Feed the mistake, and the mistake grows. A player makes an error, then spends the next ten minutes replaying it. Feeding it. And the more they feed it, the bigger it gets — until it's crowded out everything else and they've missed three more chances. Feed the doubt, and the doubt grows. Feed the "what if it goes wrong," and that grows too. But it works the other way just as well. Feed the next action, and the next action gets stronger. Feed the thing they can do, and that's what grows. So the core question in Focus is simple: What am I feeding? Not "what happened" — what am I feeding right now?

This week's challenge

When your player gets stuck on something after a game, help them catch it with one question: "Is that worth feeding?" If it's not, the skill is to stop feeding it and point the attention somewhere useful. Where is my attention right now?
