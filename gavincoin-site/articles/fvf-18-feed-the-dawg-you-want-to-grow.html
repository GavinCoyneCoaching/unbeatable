Frustration vs Fascination

Feed The DAWG You Want To Grow

Two people can go through the exact same setback and walk away with completely different outlooks. One sees a reason to quit. The other sees a reason to keep going. Same situation, different focus, different outcome. A lot of it comes down to one question.

Attention is food. Whatever you feed, grows. Which DAWG are you feeding? Not a dog — the mentality that shows up when things get hard. We've all got two voices competing for attention. One says "this is too hard." The other says "good, let's get to work." One says "you're not good enough." The other says "you're getting better." Neither voice fully disappears. They both stick around. The question is which one gets fed — because whatever gets your attention gets your energy, and whatever gets your energy tends to grow. Resilience isn't the absence of doubt. It's noticing the doubt and choosing not to feed it — redirecting the attention somewhere more useful instead.

This week's challenge

At the end of the day, ask: "Which voice got most of my attention today — and is that the one I want to grow?"
