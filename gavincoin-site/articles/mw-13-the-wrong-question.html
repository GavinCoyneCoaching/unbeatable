Mirrors & Windows — The Power of Perspective

The Wrong Question

Frustration has a favourite set of questions. Why is this happening to me? Why am I so unlucky? Why am I not good enough? They feel productive — like we're getting to the bottom of something. The quality of your answers is decided by the quality of your questions. We're not analysing the problem with those questions. We're feeding it. They all point backwards. Mirrors & Windows offers a forward-facing version of the same idea: not "why did I fail?" but "what can I improve?" Not "why am I struggling?" but "what am I missing?" And underneath all of it, the question this whole philosophy is built on: what can I control? Same situation. Different question. Different outcome.

This week's challenge

Catch one backward-pointing question from your player this week and rewrite it together, forward.
