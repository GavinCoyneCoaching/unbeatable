Mirrors & Windows — The Power of Perspective

Where Unbeatable Actually Began

Unbeatable didn't start as a business plan. It started in the car, on the way to training, with my two daughters. One of them would be nervous — a mistake she was still carrying, a stronger opponent, a bad run of form. And like most parents, my instinct was to reassure her. "You'll be fine. Don't worry about it."

Reassurance doesn't remove worry. It just tells them not to feel it. So the conversations changed. Instead of handing over answers, I started asking questions. What matters today? What can you control? What will you do after a mistake? And here's the thing that built a whole programme: she usually already had the answer. The worry wasn't a lack of ability. It was blocking her from reaching what she already knew. That's the whole idea behind Unbeatable, and it's where Mirrors & Windows comes from too. We're not filling players with something they don't have. We're helping them get back to what's already in there — when it matters most, on the far side of the white line.

This week's challenge

Next time your player is nervous before a game, resist the reassurance for one moment. Ask instead: "What's your job today?" See what comes back. More often than not, they already know.
