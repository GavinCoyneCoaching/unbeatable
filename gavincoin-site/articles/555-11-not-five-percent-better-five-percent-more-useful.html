Not Five Percent Better. Five Percent More Useful.

Across this series we've kept coming back to one question: how can I contribute more? On the surface it sounds like an individual challenge — something for one player to work on. But it was never really about the individual. It's about what happens when an entire team starts asking the same question at the same time.

Not five percent better. Five percent more useful. That's where extraordinary teams get built — not through one giant breakthrough, but through hundreds of small contributions stacking on top of each other. One extra run. One extra tackle. One extra word of encouragement. Alone, barely worth mentioning. Together, impossible to ignore. Think of a treble-winning team remembered for its trophies — and forget the depth underneath them. Someone got injured, someone stepped in. Someone had an off day, someone else covered it. No one expected to carry the team. Everyone expected to help it. Contribution creates contribution — one player communicates more, so the next one organises better, so the next one gets better ball. Most teams don't need one player to improve by 100%. They need twenty players to improve by 5%.

This week's challenge

"What could you do this week that makes a teammate's job just a little bit easier?" In the final email: the one question that ties this whole series together.
