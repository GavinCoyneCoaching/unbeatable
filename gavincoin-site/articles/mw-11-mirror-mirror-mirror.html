Mirrors & Windows — The Power of Perspective

Mirror, Mirror, Mirror

Mid-match, chaos on, a coach shouting a full paragraph of instructions from the sideline rarely lands. There's no time to process it.

One word, repeated, can do what a paragraph can't. This is where the sideline trigger-word comes in. A coach shouts "Mirror, mirror, mirror" — nothing more — and it means one thing: focus on myself, what can I do next? No lecture, no explanation needed in the moment, because the meaning was already built beforehand. It works a bit like a code word that flips a switch — say it, and the response is already loaded. The same cue works just as well from a parent in the car, or a player talking to themselves under their breath before a free.

This week's challenge

Agree one cue with your team or your child this week — "mirror" is the simplest place to start — and use it once, live, from the sideline or the stand.
