5-5-5

More Ways to Help Than You Think

Ask most players what they contribute to the team and they'll tell you their headline skill. The striker scores. The keeper saves. The big defender wins the ball. Anything outside that feels like someone else's job.

Contribution isn't one skill. It's a wheel. There are far more ways to help a team than the one thing you're known for. Communication. Effort. Encouraging a teammate who's down on themselves. Winning a throw-in nobody else chases. A defender who tracks back to do their job — and then, when the moment allows it, pushes on to offer an extra option going forward instead of just standing still once the ball's cleared. That's not abandoning their job. That's adding another way to help. That's the thinking shift underneath 5-5-5: not "what am I good at?" — but "what else could I become useful at?" It flips the question players ask themselves before every session. Instead of measuring themselves against one skill, they start looking for the next way to help.

This week's challenge

"Name one way you could contribute this week that isn't your usual job on the pitch." It doesn't have to be big. It just has to be new.
