Mirrors & Windows — The Power of Perspective

The Question That Changes Everything

Your player is up against someone quicker, stronger, more experienced. It's just not their day on that individual battle. So what happens next — do they disappear from the game, or find another way in?

Stop asking "how do I beat them?" Start asking "how can I help us?" Maybe they won't win today's matchup. But they can make the run that creates space for someone else. They can communicate, track back, support, lift a teammate after a mistake, bring energy when the team needs it most. A player's value to the team was never measured by whether they do everything. It's measured by whether they keep finding ways to help — even on the days their own battle isn't going their way.

This week's challenge

Before the next tough matchup, ask: "If today isn't your day one-on-one, how will you still help?"
