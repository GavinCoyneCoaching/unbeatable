Frustration vs Fascination

The Story You're Telling Yourself

Two players. Same missed shot. Same scoreline. Completely different stories walking off the pitch. One says "I'm useless, I always mess this up." The other says "I missed that one — what do I adjust next time?" Same event. Different story. Different week ahead.

"I can't do this" — or "I can't do this yet." A lot of what we feel isn't really the situation — it's a blend, a marble effect, between what something actually is and what we fear or hope it is. "I'm useless, I'm not good, I can't do this." Well — yes, currently you can't. But with time, effort and a bit of dedication, there's no reason that can't move from weakness, to neutral, to strength. The tool here is to try and look at it from a cold, emotionless perspective — the facts of what happened, not the emotional story stacked on top of it — so you don't end up compounding it, marbling more and more feeling into what was really just one missed shot on one day.

This week's challenge

Catch the word "can't" this week — yours or your player's — and add one word to it: "I can't do this… yet."
