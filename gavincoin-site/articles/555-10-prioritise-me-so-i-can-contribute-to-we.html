5-5-5

Prioritise Me, So I Can Contribute to We

There's a guilt that creeps in around self-investment. Taking the rest. Saying no to one more thing. Spending time on your own development instead of everyone else's. It can feel selfish — like putting yourself first means putting the team second.

You gotta prioritise me before you can contribute to we. It works the other way to how it feels. Prioritising yourself — your energy, your health, your own growth — doesn't take away from what you give to your team, your family or your work. It's what makes what you give worth something. A parent who's looked after themselves shows up with more patience on the sideline. A player who's rested shows up with more to offer at training. A stronger you strengthens every team you're on. That's the pairing at the heart of this whole idea: 5-5-5 is the outward half — expand your capacity to contribute. Prioritising yourself is the inward half — fill that capacity up in the first place. One without the other doesn't hold up for long.

This week's challenge

Two questions, side by side: "What does my team need from me? And what do I need, so I actually have that to give?"
