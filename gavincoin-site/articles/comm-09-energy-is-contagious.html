Energy Is Contagious

Have you ever walked into a room and felt the atmosphere before anyone said a word? Sport is one of the clearest places to see it. A team concedes early — one head drops, then another, the talking gets quieter, the effort fades. Nobody chose it. It just spread. It works the other way too. One player chases back. Another follows. Someone claps the effort. The scoreboard hasn't moved, but the energy has — and energy changes everything.

You can't always choose what you feel. You can choose what you contribute. Body language communicates. Effort communicates. Reactions communicate. A player jogging back sends one message; sprinting back sends another. Leaders become emotional amplifiers whether they mean to or not — people watch them, then mirror them. The question worth asking isn't what energy am I feeling right now. It's what energy am I bringing into this environment? You can be nervous and still contribute calm. Be the thermostat, not the thermometer — set the temperature in the room instead of just reflecting it back.

This week's challenge

Notice the energy you personally bring onto the pitch or sideline this week. Are you the thermostat — or just the thermometer?
