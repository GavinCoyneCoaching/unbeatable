The Stairs

Skill Set Gets You There. Mindset Keeps You There.

One of the biggest misconceptions in sport is that success happens the moment you reach the top — make the panel, become a starter, win the championship. Those moments matter. But they're rarely the end of the story. Usually they're the start of a new one. Because getting to the top is one challenge. Staying there is another.

Skill set gets you there. Mindset keeps you there. Your skill set helps you climb. Your mindset helps you stay. A player's technical ability earns them selection — but now: can they handle expectation? Recover from mistakes? Keep improving when everyone else is chasing their position? That's mindset territory. I don't think mindset is positive thinking or motivation. I think it's your ability to keep climbing when things get difficult — to stay coachable after success, to handle setbacks without abandoning the journey. Because skill alone has limits. We've all seen talented players disappear from a starting team — not because they couldn't perform, but because they couldn't sustain it. The higher you climb, the stronger the wind gets. New levels, new devils. Skill might get you there. Mindset keeps you there. What's my next step?

This week's challenge

Ask your player about the goal they're closest to reaching: "Once you get there — what will it take to stay there?" Start building that mindset before they need it, not after. In the last email: why there's no such thing as a final staircase.
