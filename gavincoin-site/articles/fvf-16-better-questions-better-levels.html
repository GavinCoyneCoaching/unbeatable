Frustration vs Fascination

Better Questions, Better Levels

"Why won't the coach pick me?" It's a completely understandable question. It's also one that keeps you exactly where you are, because there's no useful answer to it — only more frustration.

The next level isn't a better answer. It's a better question. We tend to assume progress comes from better answers — better tactics, better coaching, better preparation. All of that helps. But before any of it, we usually need a better question. "Why won't the coach pick me?" creates frustration. "What would the coach need to see to trust me more?" creates action. Notice something about the players, coaches and leaders who keep climbing: the higher they go, the more questions they ask. Not because they know less — because they know there's always more to find out.

This week's challenge

Take the frustrated question your player's been sitting with. Write it down. Then write the better version next to it: "What's the question that would actually move me forward?"
