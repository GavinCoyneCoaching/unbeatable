Communication

What Does Your Team Actually Sound Like?

We've covered a lot of ground in this series — the four layers, the message versus the meaning, silence, energy, the words that become someone's inner voice. Here's a way to find out, in your own club or your own house, which of it is actually true right now.

The fastest way to check your communication is to listen back to it. Try this over the weekend: record a full match — audio only, nothing fancy. Then actually listen back. Is it mostly encouragement and problem-solving? Or is it blame, frustration and panic? Most of us have a story in our head about which one it is. The recording tells you the truth. If you don't love what you hear, you don't need a complete overhaul. Small swaps do the work: • Add one more helpful voice than usual — yours or someone else's. • Swap one negative phrase for a better question. "What are you doing?" becomes "What did you see there?" • Remember the thermostat, not the thermometer — set the temperature, don't just reflect it. • Catch someone doing something right, out loud, before you catch someone doing something wrong.

This week's challenge

Record one match or session, audio only, and actually listen back to it before the next one. What does your team sound like right now — and is that the soundtrack you want them repeating to themselves in ten years?
