Focus

Getting Distracted Isn't the Problem

A lot of players think focus means never getting distracted. So the moment their mind wanders, they feel like they've failed — and then they beat themselves up about it, which is just another distraction stacked on top of the first.

Getting distracted isn't the failure. Staying distracted is. Everyone's attention drifts. The best players in the world get distracted. That's not the skill. The skill is the return — how quickly you notice your attention has wandered, and bring it back to the next action. Notice. Return. That's it. And like anything, the more you practise the return, the faster it gets. A player who expects to drift, and gets good at coming back, is far more focused than one who's terrified of ever losing concentration.

This week's challenge

Reassure your player that drifting is normal — then make the return the target: "How quickly did you get your attention back?" Not "did you lose focus." How fast did you find it again. Where is my attention right now?
