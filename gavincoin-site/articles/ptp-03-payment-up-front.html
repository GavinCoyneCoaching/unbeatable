Paying the Price

Payment Up Front

Last time: the bill always arrives — you only get to choose when. Here's the piece that decides how ready you are when it does. There's an old saying: success comes before work only in the dictionary. Everywhere else, the work comes first.

We want the result to come first. It never does. Action. Consistency. Effort. Intensity. All of it has to happen before the result shows up — not after. And here's the hard truth young players especially need to hear: there's no guarantee. Teams train tooth and nail all season and still don't lift the trophy. So why pay the price at all? Because paying it is the only thing that puts you in position to win when the chance comes. You don't do the work because it guarantees the result. You do it so that when the opportunity arrives, you're ready to take it.

Payment up front isn't the price of success. It's the price of entry. You want the good view of the castle, you pay at the gate. No ticket, no view. Am I paying the price, or paying the cost?

This week's challenge

Help your player do one thing before they feel ready this week — one rep, one session, one bit of preparation — with no promise of reward attached. That's the habit. That's payment up front.
