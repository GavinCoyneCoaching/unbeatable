You Can't Pour From an Empty Cup

Picture the parent who's running on empty — up early, working all day, straight to training, trying to find patience and encouragement for their kid that they honestly don't have left in the tank. Or the player who's stretched between school, sport and everything else, showing up to training with nothing in reserve. We tell ourselves the answer is to give more. Push harder. Show up anyway.

You can't pour from an empty cup. Prioritise yourself. Not so you become more important. But so you become more capable. Because the stronger version of you has more to give. That's not an excuse to switch off or stop showing up. It's the opposite — it's recognising that self-investment isn't selfish, it's the prerequisite. Rest, recovery, looking after your own energy and development — these aren't things that compete with contribution. They're what make contribution possible in the first place. Fill yourself up before you try to give to others. It sounds simple. Most of us do it backwards.

This week's challenge

"What's one thing you need to do this week to fill your own cup — before you try to give more to your team, your family, or anyone else?"
