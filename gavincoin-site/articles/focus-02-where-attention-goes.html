Focus

Where Attention Goes

Watch a player having a hard game and you'll often see the same thing — their attention is everywhere except where it needs to be. The referee. The scoreline. The mistake from five minutes ago. The parent on the sideline.

Performance follows attention. Wherever a player's attention goes, their energy follows it. And wherever their energy goes, the results show up — good or bad. If attention is on the things they can't control, the energy drains into frustration and the game drifts further away. If attention is on the next action — the thing they can do — the energy goes to work, and the performance follows. It's the quietest, fastest thing a player can control. Not their talent, not the opposition, not the conditions. Just where they point their attention. That's the idea underneath everything we teach inside Unbeatable: where our attention goes, our energy flows, and our results show.

This week's challenge

During the next game or session, ask your player one thing afterwards: "Where was your attention when it went well — and where was it when it didn't?" They'll start to notice the pattern themselves. Where is my attention right now?
