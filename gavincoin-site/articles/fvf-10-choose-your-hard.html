Frustration vs Fascination

Choose Your Hard

We quietly tell ourselves that one day it gets easier. One day the setbacks stop, the pressure eases off, the hard work becomes optional. It doesn't. The longer I coach, the more I've come to believe: everything is hard. Training is hard. Being unfit is hard. Practising the weaker foot is hard. Being predictable and limited as a player is hard too. Every path has a price on it.

There's no path without hard. Only which hard you choose. Picture ten cups of tea, and someone asking "which is the right cup?" They're all just shades of the same thing. Hard is relative — what floors one player might not register for another, and both deserve respect either way. You don't get to avoid difficulty. You get to choose your version of it. And it's not even about winning every battle. You don't need to win every confrontation, every moment — you just need to come away with a lesson. Over a season, aim for 51/49. Get slightly more right than wrong, and that's enough. That's the margin we're actually working with.

This week's challenge

When it gets tough this week — and it will — don't ask "why is this so hard?" Ask: "Which hard am I choosing here?"
