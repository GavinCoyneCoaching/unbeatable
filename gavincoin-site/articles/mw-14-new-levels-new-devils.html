Mirrors & Windows — The Power of Perspective

New Levels, New Devils

Your player finally makes the starting team. Or the team wins the championship they'd been chasing all year. And then — the struggle gets worse, not better.

New levels, new devils. Success doesn't remove problems. It changes them. A starting spot brings the pressure of keeping it. A championship brings the pressure of defending it. So many players believe reaching the next level will finally make everything easy — and every level brings a new challenge instead. That's actually good news. Struggle often isn't evidence you're failing. It's evidence you've reached the edge of your current ability — and the edge is exactly where growth happens. The players who thrive aren't the ones with the fewest problems. They're the ones growing their ability faster than their problems grow.

This week's challenge

If your player is struggling after a step up, name it plainly: "New level, new devil. That's not a step backward — it's a sign you got there."
