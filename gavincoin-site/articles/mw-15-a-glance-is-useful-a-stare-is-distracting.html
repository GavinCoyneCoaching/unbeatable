Mirrors & Windows — The Power of Perspective

A Glance Is Useful. A Stare Is Distracting.

I want to be clear about something, because it's easy to misread this idea. I'm not saying never look out the window. The best players do — they scan the pitch, notice space, read the opponent.

Looking was never the problem. Staying there is. Think about driving. You constantly check your mirrors, glance at signs. But stare at the rear- view mirror for thirty seconds and it won't end well. Sport's no different — gather the information, then return immediately to the next action. The simplest reminder I give players, three words: Glance. Learn. Return. Glance at what you need to see. Learn what you need to know. Then return to where performance actually happens — because your next decision has never been made through the window. It's always been made in the mirror.

This week's challenge

Teach the three words this week, and use them together straight after the next mistake: "Glance. Learn. Return. — go."
