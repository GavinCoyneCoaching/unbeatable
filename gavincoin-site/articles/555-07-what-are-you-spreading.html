5-5-5

What Are You Spreading?

One player gets a bad call and starts complaining. Ten minutes later, without anyone deciding it, half the team's shoulders are down and the energy in the group has changed. Or the opposite: one player claps a mistimed tackle like it was a tackle that mattered, and the whole group seems to stand a little taller. Nobody called a meeting. It just spread.

Contribution spreads. So does complaining. Culture moves through a team the same way in both directions — good habits, communication and effort spread just as easily as low effort and negativity do. That's the domino effect. One player's attitude becomes the next player's attitude becomes the group's attitude, long before anyone notices it happening. This matters for 5-5-5 because contribution isn't just what a player does with the ball. It's what they add to — or take from — the atmosphere around them. Every player is spreading something, whether they mean to or not.

This week's challenge

"What's one thing — good or bad — you've noticed spreading through your team lately? What would happen if you deliberately spread the opposite?"
