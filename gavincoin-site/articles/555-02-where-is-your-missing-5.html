5-5-5

Where Is Your Missing 5%?

Every player has a part of their game they love, and a part they quietly avoid. The one who loves getting on the ball but disappears when it comes to tracking back. The one who'll shoot from anywhere but won't say a word out loud on the pitch. The one who only ever kicks off their favoured side. We tend to spend our time on the part we're already good at. It's comfortable. It feels like progress. And it's easy to convince ourselves that's where the improvement lives.

The biggest opportunity is usually hiding in the part of the game you'd rather avoid. The instinct, when a team isn't contributing enough, is to look around. If only they communicated more. If only they tracked back. If only they worked harder. That's a window — looking at everyone else. Inside Unbeatable we teach Mirrors & Windows: instead of asking what everyone else should be doing, ask what you could do. 5-5-5 pushes that one step further — where is my missing 5%? Think of a team built around one brilliant, one-dimensional player — the opposition eventually stops trying to beat the whole team and just tries to stop him. The more complete a player is, the harder they are to stop. You're not trying to become perfect at everything. You're trying to stop your weakest area from limiting your strongest one.

This week's challenge

Ask your player — and be honest yourself, too: "What's your missing 5%? What's the part of your game you already know you're avoiding?" Just naming it is the first step.
