Frustration vs Fascination

Expect It To Be Hard

There's a particular kind of frustration that comes from being caught off guard — you walked into training, or the match, expecting it to be fine, and instead it was a slog. That extra layer of "I wasn't ready for this" makes the hard bit feel even harder.

Expect the bumpy road, and nothing catches you off guard. This isn't about doom and gloom — not "everything's going to be terrible." I just don't expect it to be easy. I know it'll be grand at the end, but I know the road to get there will have bumps in it. Go into a session or a match expecting hard, and one of two things happens. Either it's as hard as you expected — no surprise, not caught off guard, you just get on with it. Or it's easier than expected — "that wasn't so bad" — and that's a lovely feeling in itself. Either way, you win. And next time something similar comes up, you can think back: "last time I was worried about how hard it'd be, and it turned out fine — so maybe I just take a breath and relax into this one."

This week's challenge

Before the next tough session or match, say it out loud together: "I expect this to be hard. Let's see." Notice what changes when the surprise is taken out of it.
