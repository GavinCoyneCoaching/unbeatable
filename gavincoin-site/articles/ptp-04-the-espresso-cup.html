Paying the Price

The Espresso Cup

Last time: the work has to come first, with no guarantee attached. Here's what that work actually looks like, side by side. Two players go to fill a bucket from the river. One has a pint glass. The other, only an espresso cup. You'd back the pint glass every time — more capacity, more ability. But there's a catch.

It's not the size of your cup. It's how full you fill it. The player with the pint glass only ever half-fills it. Doesn't quite apply themselves. Kind of tries. The player with the espresso cup fills it to the very brim, every single time, as often as they can. And slowly, the espresso cup gets more done — because they're using everything they have, and the pint glass is wasting most of what it's got. Ability you don't use isn't ability. It's potential going cold.

And here's the reward. Fill your cup fully, often enough, and your cup gets bigger. Capacity grows through use. The reward for using your cup is a bigger cup. Am I paying the price, or paying the cost?

This week's challenge

Pick one session this week and ask your player to fill their cup to the brim — full effort, full attention, nothing held back. Not "be the best." Just — use everything you brought today.
