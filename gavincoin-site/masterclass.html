What if they're just distracted?

As a coach, you've stood on the sideline scratching your chin, wondering where it's all gone wrong – or why it hasn't clicked the way you know it can. As a parent, you've stood behind the rail watching training and matches unfold. You can see them starting to feel overwhelmed, chasing the moment rather than being ready for it when it comes. And as much as you'd love to run out and tell them it's okay, they're doing great – you can't.

So we often reach for "not talented enough" or "hasn't the head for it." But maybe it's simpler. The skill is there – they just can't get to it when their head is somewhere else. On the last mistake. The scoreboard. The voices from the sideline.

The moments in our day arrive one after another – like groceries coming down the belt at a supermarket. Whether we're focused or distracted when each one lands sends it one of two very different ways.
