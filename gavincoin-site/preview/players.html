For players

You already know what to do. The hard part is doing it when it matters.

"I knew what to do — I just didn't do it." Nearly every player says it. It's not that the skill isn't there. It's that your head got loud and you couldn't reach it. That's a thing you can train.

Ever had one of these?

The moments that trip everyone up

Minute 5's miss, still carried at minute 65. One early mistake and an hour later you're still lugging it around — flat, distracted, done. Sixty good minutes wasted paying interest on one bad one.
The run you made that nobody saw. Perfect run, no call, no pass. "Sorry, I didn't know you were there." The run only counts if someone knows about it.
"I'm useless, I always mess this up." One miss and you've written the whole story about yourself — then you go tight and small, and the story comes true.

So you reach into the bag

You don't need more information. You've got plenty. You need a fast way to the one thing that helps right now.

Frustration vs Fascination

Getting distracted isn't the failure. Staying distracted is. Notice → return.

The Jar

How much of me does this really deserve right now?

5·5·5

Your go-to's not working? Stop asking "how good am I?" — ask "how can I help right now?"

And when your coach says "focus" — focus on what? Aim it at something you can do. Next pass. Next block. Next tackle. Get the momentum back on your side.

How do you handle the big moments?
