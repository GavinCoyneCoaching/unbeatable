The Jar — Why Organisation Matters

The jar was never too small

After I shared my own story, a few people replied with the same line: "I just don't have enough time." For years, I said it too. I needed more hours, more energy, a bigger jar. But the more I reflected, the more uncomfortable the truth became: the jar was never too small. The problem was how I was filling it.

The jar was never too small. It's what filled it. The shift came when I stopped asking "How do I fit more into my day?" and started asking "What actually deserves to be in my day?" Very different questions. The first assumes the answer is more. The second forces you to think about priorities. Because our priorities are rarely revealed by what we say. They're revealed by what fills our jar. If someone looked at how you actually spend your time, what would they say your priorities are? Would it match what you claim? Our jars tell the truth. The issue was never time. It was clarity.

This week's challenge

Ask your player — or yourself — this exact question: "If someone watched how you actually spent this week, what would they say your priorities are?" Then compare that honestly to what you'd say your priorities are.
