The Jar — Why Organisation Matters

My jar was full. My priorities weren't.

A confession: this isn't my first attempt at building Unbeatable. Parts of it have sat in notebooks and folders for the best part of twenty years. And for most of those years I was busy. Up early, home late, six days a week, grinding — and somehow never quite moving forward. I assumed busy meant productive. Then a mentor asked me one question that stung: Am I being busy, or am I being productive?

Being busy and being productive are not the same thing. I'd spent years confusing movement with progress. My jar was full. My priorities weren't. I kept telling myself I needed more time, more hours, more energy. What I actually needed was better priorities — to identify the few things that truly mattered and protect them. Things changed after that. Not because I got busier. Because I got clearer. One feels like moving. The other actually moves you forward. I see the same thing in players who train constantly but don't improve, and in parents who are exhausted from running everywhere with nothing to show for the week — full jar, wrong priorities.

This week's challenge

Look back over your own week — or your player's — and ask honestly: "Was I being busy, or was I being productive?" Don't answer too quickly. Sit with it.
