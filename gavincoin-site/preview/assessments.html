Assessments

One honest look. Ten areas. A clear place to start.

Pick the one that fits you. Each takes about three minutes, gives you a score out of 100 straight away, and shows where to build first.

For players

Player Assessment

For an individual player — on their own, or with a parent or coach.

  • Focus, response to mistakes, composure
  • Habits, discipline, commitment
  • Leadership and team contribution
Start the player assessment

For coaches

Team Assessment

For a coach or management team rating one group or panel.

  • Communication, focus, response
  • Effort, standards, support & trust
  • Leadership and coachability
Start the team assessment

For clubs

Club Assessment

For a committee member or coaching officer looking across several panels.

  • Communication and focus across panels
  • Player engagement and contribution
  • Shared language and follow-through
Start the club assessment

A coaching tool, not a test. These assessments are a practical starting point for player development — not therapy, not a diagnosis. Your answers go to Gavin so he can talk them through with you if you'd like.
