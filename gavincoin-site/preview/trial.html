Day by day

The Five Days

One idea a day. Each one takes a few minutes to read and a lifetime to get good at.

Day 1

Mirrors & Windows

"Where is my attention right now?"

Stand at a window and attention goes outward — the ref, the score, the opposition. Stand at a mirror and it comes back to what you can control.

Day 2

The Jar

"Does this deserve space in my jar right now?"

Big rocks first. Not everything that feels urgent deserves your attention in this moment.

Day 3

The Stairs

"What's my next step?"

Nobody climbs a staircase by staring at the top of it. Shrink the focus to the step in front of you.

Day 4

Frustration vs Fascination

"Frustrated, or fascinated?"

Every mistake is a fork in the road. Frustration ends the conversation. Fascination starts one.

Day 5

Beyond the Scoreboard

"How was your CARE score today?"

Communication, Attitude, Resilience, Effort. Four scoreboards a player can win on any day — even the day they lose.
